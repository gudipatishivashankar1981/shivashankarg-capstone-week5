# Leave and Time-Off Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the paid and unpaid leave entitlements for all permanent employees of Meridian Digital Services ("Meridian"). It applies to full-time and part-time employees in every country where Meridian operates. Contractors, consultants and interns are governed by the leave terms in their individual agreements. Where the law of an employee's country grants a higher entitlement than this policy, the statutory entitlement prevails. Country addenda on PeopleHub list any local differences.

## 2. Annual leave

2.1 Entitlement. Every full-time employee receives 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year (1 January to 31 December). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted hours.

2.2 Accrual. Annual leave accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month. Employees who join part-way through the year receive a pro-rata entitlement from their joining date, rounded up to the nearest half day. Leave accrues from the first day of employment, including the probation period. Employees on probation may take accrued leave with their manager's approval.

2.3 Carry-forward. Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of that year, otherwise they lapse. No other leave type can be carried forward.

2.4 Encashment. Annual leave cannot be exchanged for cash during employment. On separation, unused accrued annual leave (including any carried-forward days, up to a maximum of 25 days) is paid at the employee's basic daily rate in the final settlement. If an employee has taken more leave than they have accrued, the excess is recovered in the final settlement.

2.5 Notice period. Leave taken during a notice period needs the approval of both the line manager and the HR Business Partner. Taking leave does not shorten the notice period.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, available from the first day of employment. Sick leave is not carried forward and cannot be encashed. Employees must inform their manager before the start of the working day, or as soon as practicable. A medical certificate is required if the absence exceeds 2 consecutive working days. Employees who need more than 10 days of sick leave may use accrued annual leave or apply to the HR Business Partner for extended unpaid medical leave supported by medical documentation. Confidential counselling is available through the Employee Assistance Programme described in HR-005.

## 4. Casual leave

Employees receive 5 days of casual leave per calendar year for short personal matters. Casual leave may be taken for a maximum of 2 consecutive working days at a time and, where practicable, with one working day's notice. Casual leave is not carried forward.

## 5. Parental leave

5.1 Primary caregiver. The primary caregiver receives 26 weeks of fully paid leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Leave may start up to 4 weeks before the expected date of birth.

5.2 Secondary caregiver. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of fully paid leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

5.3 Eligibility. Parental leave is available to permanent employees after 6 months of continuous service. Before that point, only the statutory entitlement of the employee's country applies.

## 6. Bereavement leave

Employees receive 5 working days of paid leave on the death of a spouse or partner, child, parent or sibling. Employees receive 2 working days on the death of a grandparent, parent-in-law or other close relative. Managers may approve additional travel time where the funeral is far away. Bereavement leave does not reduce the annual leave balance.

## 7. Public holidays and floating holidays

Meridian observes 12 public holidays per year, taken from the country calendar that HR publishes on PeopleHub each December for the following year. Each employee also receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year, which can be taken on any working day with manager approval. Floating holidays are not carried forward.

## 8. Unpaid leave

After 12 months of continuous service, an employee may request up to 3 months of unpaid leave. The request needs the approval of the Director and the HR Business Partner and at least 30 days' notice, except in emergencies. Company-paid health insurance continues during approved unpaid leave of up to 3 months.

## 9. How to apply and approve leave

Leave is applied for on PeopleHub under Leave > Apply. For absences of 3 or more consecutive working days, employees must apply at least 5 working days in advance. Managers must approve or decline within 2 working days; if they do not, the request is escalated automatically to the next-level manager. Approved leave can be cancelled up to one working day before it starts. An absence of more than 3 consecutive working days without approval is treated as absence without leave and is handled under the Code of Conduct (HR-002). Weekends and public holidays that fall inside a leave period are not counted as leave days. Leave balances on PeopleHub are refreshed on the first working day of each month.

## 10. Quarter-end freeze and delivery-critical roles

Employees named as delivery-critical in a project resource plan need Director approval, in addition to their line manager's approval, for annual leave of more than 3 working days that overlaps the quarter-end change freeze. The freeze covers the last 5 working days of March, June, September and December (see OPS-005). This rule applies only to annual leave. Sick, parental and bereavement leave are not affected.

## 11. Getting help

Questions about this policy can be sent to the HR Service Desk at hrdesk@meridian.example or raised through PeopleHub chat. Balance corrections are handled by the HR Operations team within 3 working days of the request.
