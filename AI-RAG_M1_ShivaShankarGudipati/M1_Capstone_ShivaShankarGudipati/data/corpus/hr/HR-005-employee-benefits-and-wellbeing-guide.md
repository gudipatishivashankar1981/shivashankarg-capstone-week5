# Employee Benefits and Wellbeing Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide summarises the benefits available to permanent employees of Meridian Digital Services ("Meridian"). Amounts are stated in US dollars for simplicity; the local-currency equivalents for each country are published in the country benefits schedule on PeopleHub. Where this guide and an insurer's policy wording differ, the insurer's wording governs the terms of cover.

## 2. Health insurance

2.1 Cover. Health insurance starts on the first day of employment. Meridian pays for cover for the employee, their spouse or partner and up to 2 dependent children. The sum insured is USD 20,000 per family per year.

2.2 Parents. Employees can add their parents to the policy at their own cost. Parents can be added only during the annual open enrolment window, which runs from 1 November to 30 November each year. Cover for parents begins on 1 January of the following year.

2.3 Terms of cover. Waiting periods, exclusions, treatment of pre-existing conditions and claim limits for individual treatments are set by the insurer and described in the insurer's policy wording. They are not set out in this guide. Employees with questions about a specific condition or treatment should contact the Benefits team at benefits@meridian.example.

2.4 Claims. Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals. For reimbursement claims, employees must submit documents within 30 days of discharge.

2.5 During leave. Company-paid health insurance continues during approved unpaid leave of up to 3 months (see HR-001).

## 3. Life and accident insurance

Meridian provides life insurance equal to 3 times annual base salary and accidental death and disability cover, at no cost to the employee. Employees nominate beneficiaries on PeopleHub and should review them after any major life event.

## 4. Retirement savings

Meridian contributes 6% of basic pay to the employee's retirement plan. Employees can add voluntary contributions of up to 12% of basic pay through payroll. Employer contributions vest immediately.

## 5. Learning and development

5.1 Budget. Each employee has a learning budget of USD 1,000 per calendar year to spend on external courses, conferences and certifications. The budget does not carry over to the next year.

5.2 Approval. The course must be approved by the manager before purchase. Claims are submitted through the expense system (OPS-001) with proof of payment.

5.3 Repayment. If an employee leaves within 12 months of completing a sponsored certification that cost more than USD 500, they must repay the cost of that certification in full.

5.4 Internal learning. Meridian Academy courses are free and do not use the learning budget. Employees are encouraged to complete at least 40 hours of learning a year.

## 6. Wellness

6.1 Wellness allowance. Employees can claim up to USD 400 per calendar year for gym memberships, fitness classes and approved mental-wellbeing apps. Receipts are required and claims are submitted through the expense system.

6.2 Health check-up. Every employee is entitled to a free annual preventive health check-up at a network provider.

6.3 Employee Assistance Programme. The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers confidential counselling for personal, family, financial and work-related concerns. Employees and their dependants receive 6 free sessions per issue per year, and a helpline is open 24 hours a day. Meridian does not receive the names of employees who use the EAP or details of what they discuss.

## 7. Referral bonus

Employees who refer a successful external candidate receive a referral bonus of USD 1,000. For roles designated as critical-skill roles, the bonus is USD 2,500. The bonus is paid after the referred employee has completed 6 months of service. Employees cannot receive a bonus for referring people who report to them directly, or for roles in the HR or Talent Acquisition teams.

## 8. Family and caregiving support

In addition to the parental leave in HR-001, employees can use the back-up childcare and elder-care service, which provides up to 10 subsidised days of care per year. Returning parents can request a phased return with reduced hours for up to 8 weeks, subject to manager and HR Business Partner agreement.

## 9. Open enrolment and changes

Employees can change their benefit elections during the annual open enrolment window from 1 November to 30 November. Outside that window, changes are allowed only after a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth or adoption of a child, or loss of other cover. A change must be requested within 30 days of the life event, with supporting documents.

## 10. Leaving Meridian

Health and life cover end on the employee's last working day. Employees can ask the Benefits team about continuing cover under an individual policy with the insurer. Unused learning and wellness allowances are not paid out. Referral bonuses that fall due after an employee has left are not paid.

## 11. Getting help

The Benefits team can be reached at benefits@meridian.example or through PeopleHub chat. The team aims to respond to every query within 2 working days. Claims problems that cannot be resolved with the insurer are escalated by the Benefits team within 5 working days.
