# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

Meridian Digital Services ("Meridian") believes that collaboration, client trust and mentoring are strongest when teams spend time together, and that flexibility helps people do their best work. This policy defines the standard hybrid model, the rules for fully remote roles and the rules for working from another location for a short period. It applies to all permanent employees whose role can be performed away from a Meridian office or client site.

## 2. Standard hybrid model

2.1 Office attendance. Employees are expected to work from a Meridian office or client site for at least 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are team core days, and employees should plan to be on site on both of them. The remaining day or days can be chosen by agreement within the team.

2.2 Core hours. Wherever they work, employees must be available for meetings and collaboration between 10:00 and 16:00 in their local time zone.

2.3 What counts as an office day. Days spent at a client site and days spent travelling on approved business travel count as office days.

2.4 Team-level exceptions. A team may agree a different attendance pattern for operational reasons, for example shift-based support teams. Any exception needs the written approval of the Director and must be recorded on PeopleHub.

## 3. Fully remote arrangements

A fully remote arrangement is the exception, not the norm. It needs the approval of the Director and the HR Business Partner, and it is available only where the role does not need regular on-site presence and the employee lives in a country where Meridian has a legal entity. Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 12 months. Fully remote employees may be asked to attend up to 4 on-site team gatherings a year, with travel reimbursed under OPS-001.

## 4. Work from Anywhere

4.1 Allowance. Each employee may work from a location other than their usual place of work for up to 15 working days per calendar year, within their own country or abroad. Manager approval is required for every request.

4.2 International requests. Working from another country needs at least 4 weeks' notice. The request must also be approved by the HR Business Partner, with Tax and Legal review. This review checks right-to-work rules, tax exposure and the risk of creating a taxable presence for Meridian in that country. International Work from Anywhere is allowed only in countries on the approved list published on PeopleHub, which includes all countries where Meridian has a legal entity.

4.3 Client restrictions. Some client contracts require that work is done only from approved locations. Employees must check with their Delivery Manager before submitting a request.

4.4 Not a substitute for leave. Work from Anywhere days are working days. Employees who want to take leave while abroad must apply for leave separately under HR-001.

## 5. Security requirements when working remotely

Working remotely must not weaken the protection of Meridian or client data. Employees must use their Meridian-managed laptop and must connect through the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems, as described in IT-002. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN active. Employees must not work from countries on the restricted-travel list, where VPN access is blocked by default; the loaner-device process in IT-003 applies if business travel to those countries is unavoidable. Confidential and Restricted information (see IT-006) must not be printed at home or left visible to family members or visitors.

## 6. Home office support

6.1 Allowance. Employees on a hybrid or remote arrangement can claim a one-time home-office setup allowance of USD 300 (or the local-currency equivalent). The claim must be submitted within 90 days of joining or of the arrangement being approved. Employees can also claim a monthly connectivity allowance of USD 25. Both are claimed through the expense system described in OPS-001.

6.2 Equipment. Employees can request an external monitor, keyboard and mouse once every 3 years through ServiceHub, as set out in IT-003.

## 7. Performance and visibility

Remote and hybrid employees are assessed against the same goals and standards as office-based colleagues (see HR-004). Managers must set clear outcomes and check in regularly, and must not use hours online as a measure of performance. Employees should keep their calendar up to date so teammates know when they are available.

## 8. Changes and breaches

Meridian may change a hybrid or remote arrangement with 30 days' notice for business reasons, including client requirements or performance concerns. Repeated failure to meet the attendance expectation without an agreed exception is handled by the line manager first and may be escalated to the HR Business Partner. Misuse of Work from Anywhere, for example working from an unapproved country, is a breach of the Code of Conduct (HR-002) and can lead to disciplinary action.

## 9. Frequently asked questions

Can I work from home five days a week? Only with an approved fully remote arrangement under section 3. Can I take my laptop abroad on holiday? Business laptops can travel to approved countries only. Do I need approval to work from another city in my own country? Yes, manager approval under section 4.1, and the days count towards the 15-day allowance.
