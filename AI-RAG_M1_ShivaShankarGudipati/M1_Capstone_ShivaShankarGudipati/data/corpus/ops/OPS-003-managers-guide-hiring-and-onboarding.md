# Manager's Guide to Hiring and Onboarding

## 1. Purpose and scope

This guide helps people managers at Meridian Digital Services ("Meridian") hire, onboard and offboard team members consistently and fairly. It covers raising a requisition, running interviews, making an offer, preparing for a new joiner, probation and leaving. It applies to all managers who hire permanent employees.

## 2. Raising a requisition

Managers raise a requisition in Talent Hub, stating the role, grade, location, justification and budget source. Finance confirms the budget, and the Director and the HR Business Partner approve the requisition before sourcing starts. The target time to fill a position is 45 days from the date that the requisition is approved. Internal candidates must be considered first, and open roles are advertised internally for at least 5 working days before external advertising begins.

## 3. Interviewing and selection

3.1 Process. Every hire has at least 3 interview rounds: a recruiter screen, a technical or functional interview, and an interview with the hiring manager. For grade L6 and above, an independent senior interviewer from another team (a bar-raiser) is added.

3.2 Interviewers. At least 2 of the interviewers on each panel must have completed interviewer training. Interviewers use the structured scorecard in Talent Hub and submit feedback within 1 working day of each interview.

3.3 Fairness. Interviewers must not ask about age, marital status, family plans, religion, health or other protected characteristics. Anyone with a personal relationship with a candidate must declare it and step out of the process (HR-002).

## 4. Offers and background checks

4.1 Offer approval. An offer within the approved salary band is approved by the HR Business Partner and the Director. An offer above the band maximum also needs a Vice President and the compensation committee.

4.2 Background verification. All offers are conditional on background verification, which checks identity, employment history, education and, for client-facing roles, criminal records. It normally takes 7 to 10 working days. Candidates may start while the check is in progress, but it must be cleared within 30 days of joining, and joiners are told that the offer will be withdrawn if it is not.

## 5. Preparing for the new joiner

5.1 Order equipment and access early. The manager must order the laptop and raise all the required access requests at least 10 working days before the start date. This allows the laptop to be delivered on day one (IT-003) and access requests, which have a target of 3 working days once approved, to be fulfilled (IT-001).

5.2 Welcome. The manager assigns a buddy, books the first-week meetings, adds the joiner to the team channels and shares the first-week schedule at least 2 working days before the joining date.

5.3 Mandatory training. New joiners must complete the Code of Conduct, information security awareness and data privacy training within their first 30 days (HR-002, IT-006). Access to client production environments is not granted until the training is complete.

## 6. The first 90 days

By the end of the first week, the manager and the joiner agree a 30-60-90 day plan that sets learning goals and early deliverables. The manager holds a one-to-one at least once a week during the first month and every two weeks after that. The joiner's goals for the performance cycle are set with the manager in the first month (HR-004). Joiners who start on or after 1 October are not rated in that year's performance cycle.

## 7. Probation

The probation period is 6 months for all new permanent employees. The manager holds a confirmation review in month 5 and records the outcome in Talent Hub. Probation can be extended once by a maximum of 3 months if there are specific performance concerns; the extension needs the approval of the HR Business Partner, and the employee must be told the reasons in writing. During probation, employees accrue annual leave from day one and may take it with manager approval (HR-001).

## 8. Resignations and offboarding

8.1 Notice period. The standard notice period is 60 days for grades L1 to L5 and 90 days for grade L6 and above. A shorter notice period through a buy-out needs the approval of the HR Business Partner and the Director.

8.2 Knowledge transfer. The manager agrees a knowledge-transfer plan within 5 working days of receiving a resignation. It lists open work, key contacts, documents and access to hand over. Employees must not be given new long-term responsibilities during the notice period.

8.3 Exit interview. HR holds an exit interview with every leaver.

8.4 Devices and access. All devices must be returned on or before the last working day (IT-003). Access to all systems is switched off by the end of the last working day (IT-001).

8.5 Final settlement. Final settlement, including payment for unused annual leave of up to 25 days, is processed in line with HR-001.

## 9. Referrals

Employees can refer candidates through Talent Hub. The referral bonus, and the conditions, are explained in HR-005. Managers must not pressure employees to refer people.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

How long should it take to fill a role? 45 days is the target. When must I order a new joiner's laptop? At least 10 working days before the start date. How long is probation? 6 months, with a review in month 5. What is the notice period for an L4 employee? 60 days.
