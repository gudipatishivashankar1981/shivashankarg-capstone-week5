# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains what business travel and expenses Meridian Digital Services ("Meridian") reimburses, how they are approved, and how to claim them. It applies to all employees. Employees are expected to spend company money as carefully as they would spend their own, and managers are responsible for approving only expenses that are reasonable and business-related.

## 2. Approval before travel

All business travel must be approved in TravelDesk before anything is booked. Domestic travel needs the approval of the line manager. International travel needs the approval of the line manager and of the Director. Employees who book first and seek approval later may not be reimbursed. Travel must also respect the security rules on restricted countries in IT-003 and the Work from Anywhere rules in HR-003, where relevant.

## 3. Booking

3.1 Flights. All flights are booked through TravelDesk. Economy class is used for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class is allowed only for flights of 10 hours or more and needs the approval of a Vice President.

3.2 Hotels. Hotels are booked through TravelDesk. Nightly room rates, including taxes, are capped by city tier: Tier 1 is USD 250, Tier 2 is USD 160 and Tier 3 is USD 110. Tier 1 cities are London, New York, San Francisco, Singapore and Zurich. Tier 2 cities are Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Dublin, Toronto and Sydney. All other cities, including Pune, Chennai and Hyderabad, are Tier 3. Stays above the cap need Director approval before booking.

3.3 Rail and ground transport. Standard class rail is used for journeys under 4 hours. Employees should use ride-hailing services or public transport and should not hire cars unless there is no practical alternative. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the rate in the country schedule.

## 4. Daily meal allowance

While travelling overnight, employees can claim a daily meal allowance based on the destination city tier: USD 70 for Tier 1, USD 50 for Tier 2 and USD 35 for Tier 3. The allowance covers breakfast, lunch, dinner and incidental drinks. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## 5. Other reimbursable expenses

Reasonable costs for airport transfers, baggage fees, visas, internet access needed for work and business phone calls are reimbursable with a receipt. Home-office and connectivity allowances for hybrid employees (HR-003), learning budget purchases (HR-005) and wellness claims (HR-005) are claimed through the same expense system, but follow their own limits.

## 6. Client entertainment and gifts

Entertainment of clients, for example a business meal, is limited to USD 100 per person per event and needs the approval of the Director before the event. Gifts to clients are limited to USD 50 per person per calendar year and must be recorded in the gift register. No gift or hospitality may be offered to a public official without Legal approval (HR-002).

## 7. Submitting an expense claim

7.1 Deadline. Claims must be submitted in the expense system within 30 days of the end of the trip or of the date the expense was incurred. Late claims are rejected unless a Vice President approves an exception.

7.2 Receipts. An itemised receipt is needed for every expense over USD 25. For expenses of USD 25 or less, a description is enough.

7.3 Approval. The line manager approves each claim within 5 working days. Claims totalling more than USD 2,500 also need Director approval. Finance may audit a random 10% of claims, and employees must be able to provide original receipts if asked.

7.4 Payment. Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days of final approval, to the bank account recorded on PeopleHub.

## 8. Travel advances and corporate cards

Employees can request a travel advance of up to 50% of the estimated cost of a trip. The advance must be settled within 15 days of returning from the trip, and any unspent balance must be repaid. Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than 4 times a year, and they may only be used for business expenses. A lost or stolen corporate card must be reported to Finance Operations within 24 hours.

## 9. Expenses that are not reimbursable

Meridian does not reimburse fines and penalties, minibar and in-room entertainment, alcohol, personal grooming, travel insurance for personal trips, or expenses for family members who accompany the employee. Claims that include false or inflated amounts are a breach of the Code of Conduct (HR-002) and may lead to dismissal.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

How long do I have to claim? 30 days. When do I need receipts? For anything over USD 25. Who approves my claim? Your manager, and your Director if the total is over USD 2,500. What is the hotel limit in London? USD 250 per night, because London is a Tier 1 city. What is the meal allowance in Pune? USD 35 per day, because Pune is a Tier 3 city. Can I fly business class to New York from Mumbai? Only if the flight is 10 hours or more and a Vice President approves it.
