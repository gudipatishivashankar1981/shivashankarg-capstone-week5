# Procurement and Vendor Approval Guidelines

## 1. Purpose and scope

These guidelines explain how Meridian Digital Services ("Meridian") buys goods and services, who must approve each purchase, and how vendors are checked before they are used. They apply to every purchase made on behalf of Meridian, whatever the budget source, including software subscriptions, consulting, hardware, events and subcontractors engaged on client projects. Following the process protects Meridian from fraud, overspending, security risks and legal exposure.

## 2. Approval thresholds

Approval depends on the total value of the purchase. For recurring services, the value is the total annual contract value. For multi-year contracts, it is the total value over the whole term.

Up to USD 1,000: the line manager approves.

USD 1,001 to USD 10,000: the Director and Procurement approve.

USD 10,001 to USD 50,000: a Vice President, Procurement and Finance approve.

Above USD 50,000: the Chief Financial Officer or the Executive Committee approves, and a formal Request for Proposal (RFP) is required.

Approvals must be recorded in ProcureHub before any commitment is made to a vendor. Verbal agreements or emailed confirmation do not replace an approval.

## 3. Competitive quotes

For purchases of more than USD 5,000, the requester must obtain at least 3 written quotes from different vendors and record them in ProcureHub. Where a purchase is supplied by only one vendor, the requester must submit a sole-source justification explaining why no alternative exists. The justification needs the approval of Procurement and, for amounts above USD 10,000, of Finance.

## 4. Splitting purchases

Splitting a purchase into smaller parts to stay below an approval threshold or to avoid obtaining quotes is prohibited. Procurement monitors repeated purchases from the same vendor and will refer suspected splitting to the Ethics Office. It is a breach of the Code of Conduct (HR-002).

## 5. Vendor onboarding and due diligence

5.1 New vendors. A vendor must be onboarded by Procurement before the first order is placed. Onboarding includes a financial check, sanctions and anti-corruption screening, a conflict-of-interest declaration by the requester, and a security questionnaire for any vendor that will process, store or access Meridian or client data. Onboarding normally takes 10 working days.

5.2 Software and cloud services. Software and SaaS purchases must follow the Software Request Procedure in IT-004 first. Information Security reviews the vendor before Procurement gives final approval.

5.3 Data protection. A vendor that handles personal data must sign a data processing agreement before it receives any data. The Privacy Office maintains the template.

5.4 Preferred vendors. Where Meridian has a preferred-vendor agreement, the requester must use it unless Procurement approves an exception.

## 6. Conflicts of interest and gifts

Anyone involved in selecting a vendor must declare any personal or financial relationship with a bidder before the evaluation starts and must step back from the decision if a conflict exists (see HR-002). While a procurement is open, evaluators and other members of the project team must not accept any gift or hospitality from a bidder, regardless of its value. Bidders who offer gifts may be excluded from the process. Any approach by a vendor outside the formal process should be reported to Procurement.

## 7. Contracts

Only authorised signatories may sign contracts on behalf of Meridian. Line managers, project managers and other employees cannot sign vendor agreements unless they hold written delegated authority. Legal must review every contract with a value above USD 10,000 and any contract that has non-standard terms, regardless of value. Contracts with auto-renewal clauses need a renewal reminder in ProcureHub at least 90 days before the renewal date.

## 8. Purchase orders and payment

Employees must obtain a purchase order (PO) in ProcureHub before ordering. The rule is no PO, no pay: Finance does not pay invoices that do not match an approved PO. The standard payment term is 45 days from receipt of a correct invoice. Requesters must confirm receipt of goods or services in ProcureHub within 5 working days so that the invoice can be paid on time.

## 9. Emergency purchases

If a purchase is needed urgently to protect people, clients or business continuity, the requester can proceed after verbal approval from the appropriate approver. The approval must be recorded in ProcureHub within 3 working days, together with an explanation of why the normal process could not be followed. Repeated emergency purchases by the same team are reviewed by Procurement.

## 10. Subcontractors on client projects

Subcontractors engaged on a client project are vendors, and they follow this procedure. In addition, the Delivery Manager must confirm that the client contract allows subcontracting, and that the subcontractor has passed background checks and completed security training before it gets access to any client environment (see IT-001).

## 11. Frequently asked questions

Who approves a USD 8,000 purchase? The Director and Procurement; and at least 3 written quotes are needed because it is above USD 5,000. Who approves a USD 12,000 subscription? A Vice President, Procurement and Finance, and Legal must review the contract. Can I accept a gift from a bidder? Not while a procurement is open, whatever its value. How long is vendor onboarding? Normally 10 working days.
