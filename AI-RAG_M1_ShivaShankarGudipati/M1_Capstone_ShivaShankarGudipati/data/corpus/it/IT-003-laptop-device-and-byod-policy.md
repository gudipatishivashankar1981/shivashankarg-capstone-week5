# Laptop, Device and BYOD Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how Meridian Digital Services ("Meridian") issues, secures, replaces and recovers the devices employees use for work, and what personal devices may be used. It applies to all employees and contractors who use a Meridian-issued laptop, phone or tablet, or who use a personal device to access Meridian services.

## 2. Standard issue and refresh

2.1 Laptops. Each employee receives a managed laptop that matches their role. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years. Engineers and data professionals who need a high-performance workstation receive a refresh every 3 years.

2.2 New joiners. New joiners receive their laptop on their first day if the hiring manager has ordered it at least 10 working days before the start date (see OPS-003). Late orders can lead to a delay.

2.3 Peripherals. Each employee can request an external monitor, keyboard and mouse once every 3 years through ServiceHub. Home-office allowances are covered by HR-003.

2.4 Ownership. All Meridian-issued devices remain the property of Meridian. Employees must look after them and must not lend them to family, friends or other people.

## 3. Security configuration

3.1 Encryption. Full-disk encryption is mandatory on every laptop and cannot be disabled by the user.

3.2 Updates. Operating-system and security updates are installed automatically. Users can postpone an update for a maximum of 3 days; after that the device restarts to install it. Critical security patches are installed within 7 days of release.

3.3 Software. Only software from Software Center or approved through IT-004 may be installed. Users do not have local administrator rights by default.

3.4 Removable media. USB storage is disabled by default. An exception needs a request in ServiceHub with a business reason and the approval of the user's Director. Exceptions last for a maximum of 90 days.

3.5 Screen lock. Devices lock automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity. Users must lock their screen whenever they leave a device unattended.

## 4. Lost or stolen devices

4.1 Report immediately. An employee whose device is lost or stolen must report it to the IT Helpdesk and to Security Operations within 2 hours of discovering the loss. Reports can be made by phone to the 24-hour security hotline, which is the fastest way, or by ServiceHub. The delay in reporting is itself a security risk, so the 2-hour limit applies even at night and at weekends.

4.2 What happens next. Security Operations will lock the device and wipe it remotely, revoke the user's sessions and certificates and assess what data was on it. If Confidential or Restricted data may have been exposed, the incident is handled as a suspected data breach under IT-006.

4.3 Theft. If the device was stolen, the employee must also file a police report within 24 hours and send a copy to Security Operations. Employees who are abroad should contact the local police.

4.4 Replacement. The IT Helpdesk will issue a replacement loaner laptop on the next business day.

4.5 Cost. The first accidental loss or damage in a device's life is covered by Meridian. Repeated incidents or damage caused by negligence may be recovered from the employee up to 25% of the replacement cost, after a review by the employee's Director and HR.

## 5. Personal devices (BYOD)

5.1 Permitted devices. Personal phones and tablets may be used to read email, calendar and chat only if they are enrolled in Meridian's mobile device management (MDM) service. Enrolment creates a separate work container on the device, which Meridian can wipe remotely. Meridian cannot see personal photos, messages or apps.

5.2 Not permitted. Personal laptops and desktops must not be used to access internal systems or connect to the VPN. They must not be used to store or process Meridian or client data.

5.3 Requirements. Enrolled devices must have a passcode, be encrypted and run a supported operating system version. Jailbroken or rooted devices are blocked.

5.4 Leaving. When an employee leaves Meridian, the work container is removed from their personal device on their last working day.

## 6. Travel and loaner devices

Employees travelling on business to a country on the restricted-travel list must not take their usual laptop. They must request a loaner laptop through ServiceHub at least 10 working days before departure. Loaner devices have a minimal configuration and no stored client data. They are wiped on return.

## 7. Returning devices

All Meridian devices, accessories and access cards must be returned on or before the employee's last working day. Managers should include this in the offboarding plan (OPS-003). Devices that are not returned within 5 working days of the last working day are reported as lost, and the cost of replacement may be recovered from the final settlement.

## 8. Support

Hardware faults are reported through ServiceHub or by phone. A laptop that cannot be repaired the same day is replaced with a loaner on the next business day. Service levels are described in IT-005.

## 9. Frequently asked questions

I think my laptop was stolen last night; how long do I have? Report to the Helpdesk and Security Operations within 2 hours of discovering it and, for theft, file a police report within 24 hours. Can I check work email on my own phone? Yes, if the phone is enrolled in MDM. Can I plug in a USB drive? Not without an approved exception.
